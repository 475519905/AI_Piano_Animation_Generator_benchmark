# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-09

Initial public MIT release of the independent piano-animation evaluation protocol.

- Explicit unaligned point/rotation, fingering, onset and reference-assigned key-contact proxy measurements.
- Seconds-based derivative units, strict clocks/IDs, missingness and no universal quality gate.
- Full-performance macro/micro aggregation and split-declaration checks.
- Synthetic generator and 57 tests; no real-model performance claims or private data.
- No inference/training code, models, recordings, rigs, commercial assets or reverse-engineering materials.
